Why not just ask ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is excellent for explaining tax ideas. Kapital is built for one narrow job: turning the Groww export you already have into numbers you can check, file and share — without your file leaving this browser.

The short answer

01 · Privacy

Your file stays on your computer

A chatbot needs your export uploaded to its servers before it can read it. Kapital opens the file inside this browser tab. There is no server to send it to.

02 · Trust

Same file, same answer

Chat answers can change between conversations, so you have to re-check the maths. Kapital applies fixed FIFO and tax rules every time, and the code is public.

03 · Speed

No prompt writing, no waiting

You do not need to explain what each column means. Drop the CSV or Excel file you download from Groww and read the dashboard in seconds.

ChatGPT may handle a simple P&L question well. The point is not that it is always wrong; the point is that a financial calculation you can file should be repeatable, auditable and private.

Where ChatGPT is the better choice

Use ChatGPT to understand. Use Kapital to calculate.

ChatGPT is better for explaining what STCG and LTCG mean, talking through a tax idea, or drafting a note to your CA. Kapital will not chat with you and does not give advice. It takes your file and returns the same numbers every time.

What you get

Everything below comes from the files you drop in. No live prices are fetched.

  • Realized P&L with FIFO lot matching, charges and split/bonus handling
  • Unrealized P&L from holdings exports
  • Indian financial-year breakdown (April–March)
  • XIRR across buys, sells and open holdings
  • STCG/LTCG estimates, including the 23 Jul 2024 regime change
  • Per-instrument and open-holdings tables
  • A shareable PNG summary with a QR link back to the app

Supported: Groww P&L report, capital gains report, holdings export, equity tradebook and mutual fund statement.

You do not have to take our word for it

  1. Open your browser’s developer tools (press F12) and choose the Network tab.
  2. Load your Groww export in the app.
  3. Watch the requests. None of them carry your file, its name or the numbers calculated from it.
What requests does the page make?

Only the page’s own files: this page, the stylesheet, the app’s scripts and the chart/Excel libraries. The site also loads Cloudflare Web Analytics for anonymous page-view counts; it does not receive file contents, filenames or results. There is no backend.

The full source is on GitHub, MIT licensed.

Honest limits

  • Estimates, not tax advice. Verify the numbers with your chartered accountant before filing.
  • No live prices. Unrealized P&L uses the prices in your holdings export.
  • Groww exports only for now. Zerodha, Upstox and others are on the roadmap.
  • It is a calculator, not a chatbot. It will not answer general questions or give investment advice.

Common questions

Can I just upload my Groww CSV to ChatGPT?

You can, and it may do a reasonable job. But your holdings leave your device, you have to check every number, and the answer can change between chats. Kapital runs the same fixed calculation on the same file.

Does Kapital upload anything?

No. Parsing, calculation, charts and the share image all run in your browser. There is no backend. The only external requests are page assets and anonymous page views.

Is Kapital free?

Yes. It is free, needs no account, and the code is MIT licensed.

Are the tax numbers exact?

They are estimates. The app handles the 23 Jul 2024 STCG/LTCG change and lets you edit rates and exemptions, but confirm the final figures with your CA.

What if I do not use Groww?

Groww exports are the only supported format for now. The parser is open source if someone wants to add another broker.
