    How you can verify nothing is uploaded

    Open your browser’s developer tools, switch to the Network tab, then load your exports. You will see no requests carrying your data: parsing, calculation, charts and the share image are all done in this tab. The only network requests are for the page’s own assets. The only thing recorded is an anonymous page-view count (Cloudflare Web Analytics, no cookies) — never your files, filenames or their contents.
